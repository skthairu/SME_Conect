BIASHARA NDOGO ASSOCIATION OF KENYA (BNAK)

REPLIT DEVELOPMENT BRIEF — DIGITAL PLATFORM

Build a modern, scalable and professional digital platform for Biashara Ndogo Association of Kenya (BNAK).

The platform must be designed as a national digital economic ecosystem for Biashara Ndogo, not simply as an association information website.

The platform should be:

KENYAN 🇰🇪
AFRICAN 🌍
INCLUSIVE 🤝
MODERN 💻
ENTREPRENEURIAL 🚀
PROFESSIONAL

CORE BRAND DIRECTION

ROOTED IN KENYA. INSPIRED BY AFRICA. CONNECTED TO THE WORLD.

The entire platform should have a strong Kenyan and African identity.

Use authentic imagery and design elements representing Kenyan entrepreneurs, traders, farmers, markets, youth, women, artisans, boda boda entrepreneurs, small manufacturers and entrepreneurs with disabilities.

Avoid making the website look like a generic NGO, government portal or international corporate template.

It should feel like a powerful African business platform built in Kenya for entrepreneurs.

⸻

KENYAN FLAG DESIGN LANGUAGE

Incorporate the colours of the Kenyan national flag throughout the UI:

BLACK • RED • GREEN • WHITE

Use these colours elegantly as:

* Thin borders
* Page boundaries
* Section dividers
* Card outlines
* Navigation accents
* Buttons
* Progress indicators
* Icons
* Homepage design elements

The Kenyan colours should create a subtle national visual frame/boundary around the platform.

Do not overuse the colours.

The result should be premium, modern and recognisably Kenyan, rather than looking like the flag has simply been placed on the website.

⸻

PLATFORM STRUCTURE

1. BIASHARA FINANCE

Create a dedicated financial-services section connecting entrepreneurs with legitimate financial providers.

Include:

* Banks
* SACCOs
* Microfinance
* Fintechs
* Government enterprise funds
* Grants
* Asset financing
* Insurance
* Savings and investment opportunities

Finance Finder

Allow users to search/filter financial opportunities based on:

* Business type
* Location
* Financing amount
* Purpose
* Youth
* Women
* Persons with disabilities
* Agriculture
* Start-ups
* Existing businesses

Important

BNAK must NOT present itself as a lender unless it becomes legally licensed to provide lending services.

The platform should operate as a trusted information, discovery and connection platform linking entrepreneurs with legitimate providers.

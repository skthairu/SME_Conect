💰 E-MOBILITY FINANCE FINDER

This could be one of the strongest features.

A user selects:

What do you want?

🚌 Bus
🛺 Tuk-tuk
🏍️ Boda
🚚 Commercial vehicle

Then:

How do you want to acquire it?

* Financing
* Lease-to-own
* Asset finance
* Cash purchase
* Fleet financing
* Partner programme

The system then displays available verified financing/provider programmes, eligibility, deposit, repayment structure, requirements and application process.

BNAK should clearly act as a connector/information platform, unless it has the necessary licence to provide financing itself.

⸻

📋 “APPLY NOW”

Every participating provider can have its own application pathway.

For example:

APPLY FOR E-BODA

1. Select vehicle
2. Select county
3. Enter business/transport experience
4. Select financing preference
5. Complete eligibility questions
6. Submit application
7. Receive application/reference number
8. Track status

Where possible, the platform should send the application to the actual financing/provider institution, rather than making BNAK unnecessarily responsible for approving finance.

⚡ ADDITIONAL E-MOBILITY FEATURES

Expand the BNAK E-Mobility section into a complete transport and e-mobility business ecosystem, not just a vehicle application page.

1. “WHICH EV IS RIGHT FOR ME?”

Create an interactive questionnaire that considers:

* Budget
* Daily travel distance
* Passenger or cargo use
* Location
* Charging access
* Current vehicle
* Expected daily income
* Business/transport needs

The system should recommend suitable options such as:

* E-Boda
* E-TukTuk
* E-Bus / E-Matatu
* Electric commercial vehicle

⸻

2. E-MOBILITY CALCULATOR

Create tools that allow users to compare:

* Fuel versus electricity costs
* Daily operating costs
* Monthly operating costs
* Estimated savings
* Financing repayment
* Maintenance costs
* Break-even period
* Estimated total cost of ownership

The platform should clearly label calculations as estimates where applicable.

⸻

3. E-MOBILITY FINANCE MARKETPLACE

Allow participating finance providers to list their e-mobility financing products.

Users should be able to compare:

* Deposit
* Repayment period
* Eligibility
* Required documents
* Insurance requirements
* Battery terms
* Warranty
* Financing structure
* Application process
* Official application link

BNAK should act as a trusted information and connection platform unless it is legally licensed to provide financing itself.

⸻

4. BATTERY CENTRE 🔋

Create a dedicated battery information section covering:

* Battery ownership
* Battery subscription
* Battery leasing
* Battery swapping
* Battery charging
* Battery warranty
* Battery replacement
* Battery health
* Approved battery/service providers
* Battery safety and maintenance education

⸻

5. CHARGING & BATTERY-SWAP NETWORK 🗺️

Create:

“FIND CHARGING NEAR ME”

The future map should show:

* EV charging stations
* Battery-swapping stations
* Locations
* Operating hours
* Vehicle compatibility
* Available services
* Official pricing where available

Design the architecture so additional charging networks can be added later.

⸻

6. E-MOBILITY MARKETPLACE

Create a verified marketplace where approved providers can list:

* E-Bodas
* E-TukTuks
* E-Buses
* Electric commercial vehicles
* Chargers
* Batteries
* Spare parts
* Accessories
* Maintenance services

All listings should clearly identify the provider and verification status.

⸻

7. E-MOBILITY FLEET MANAGER

For entrepreneurs operating multiple electric vehicles, create:

“MY E-MOBILITY FLEET”

Allow fleet owners to manage:

* Vehicles
* Drivers/riders
* Revenue
* Energy costs
* Maintenance
* Financing
* Insurance
* Battery status
* Vehicle utilisation
* Service schedules

⸻

8. E-MOBILITY JOBS & SKILLS

Create a dedicated employment and skills section for:

* EV mechanics
* Battery technicians
* Charging technicians
* Drivers
* E-Boda riders
* E-TukTuk operators
* Sales agents
* Fleet managers
* Software and technology jobs
* Apprenticeships
* Training programmes

Connect this strongly with YOUTH BIASHARA.

⸻

9. E-MOBILITY BUSINESS OPPORTUNITIES

Show opportunities beyond vehicle ownership.

Include:

* Charging stations
* Battery-swapping businesses
* EV repairs
* Spare-parts businesses
* Vehicle dealerships
* Fleet management
* Delivery businesses
* Solar charging
* Battery services
* E-mobility technology
* Financing partnerships

The platform should help entrepreneurs see the entire e-mobility value chain.

⸻

10. E-MOBILITY GOVERNMENT & POLICY CENTRE

Create a trusted information centre for:

* E-mobility regulations
* Government programmes
* Incentives
* Standards
* Licensing requirements
* Public consultations
* Policy developments
* Official announcements

Every important piece of information should display:

SOURCE + DATE UPDATED

Avoid presenting unverified information as official government policy.

⸻

11. E-MOBILITY APPLICATION TRACKER

When a user applies for an EV or financing programme, provide a tracking system:

APPLICATION SUBMITTED
↓
VERIFICATION
↓
FINANCING REVIEW
↓
APPROVED / NOT APPROVED
↓
VEHICLE ALLOCATION
↓
DELIVERY

Users should receive:

* Application/reference number
* Status updates
* Notifications
* Required next steps
* Provider contact information

Where applications are handled by external providers, clearly distinguish the provider’s process from BNAK’s role.

⸻

12. E-MOBILITY COMMUNITY

Create a moderated community for:

* E-Boda riders
* E-TukTuk operators
* E-Bus operators
* Fleet owners
* Mechanics
* Charging operators
* Entrepreneurs
* E-mobility service providers

Allow knowledge sharing, questions, experiences and business networking while maintaining strong moderation against scams, misinformation and abuse.

⸻

⭐ 13. “START AN E-MOBILITY BUSINESS” WIZARD

Make this one of the signature features.

A user selects:

My capital → My county → My business idea → My transport needs

The platform then provides a personalised pathway showing:

* Recommended business models
* Suitable vehicle/equipment
* Estimated setup requirements
* Indicative costs
* Financing opportunities
* Training requirements
* Relevant licences/requirements
* Charging requirements
* Maintenance requirements
* Potential revenue models
* Relevant BNAK programmes
* Available opportunities

The system should clearly distinguish estimates, third-party information and official requirements.

⸻

🇰🇪 BNAK E-MOBILITY HUB

Position the entire section as:

OWN • FINANCE • CHARGE • EARN • SERVICE • GROW

The E-Mobility Hub should integrate directly with:

BODA BIASHARA
↓
E-MOBILITY
↓
BIASHARA FINANCE
↓
YOUTH BIASHARA
↓
JOBS & SKILLS
↓
BUSINESS OPPORTUNITIES

The objective is to make BNAK a platform where an entrepreneur can enter, finance, operate, maintain and grow within Kenya’s emerging electric-mobility economy.
